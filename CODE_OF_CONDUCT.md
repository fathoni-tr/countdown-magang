# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and the site's chat.

## In short

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, never attacks on the person.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual content, or sharing other people's private information.
- Welcome newcomers — many people here are students and interns making their first contribution.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **fathonitr@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so.
